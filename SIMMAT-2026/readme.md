# SIMMAT — Simulation Tools for Materials Design

This folder contains tutorials prepared for the **SIMMAT Erasmus+ KA171 School — Simulation Tools for Materials Design: An Introduction**.

SIMMAT is a three-day international training program designed for senior undergraduate students, graduate students, and PhD researchers who are interested in modern computational approaches for materials research. The program introduces several important simulation methods, including **Density Functional Theory (DFT), Machine Learning, and Transport Modelling**, with a focus on understanding and predicting material properties from the atomic scale to macroscopic behavior.

The program is organized within the **Erasmus+ KA171 International Credit Mobility** collaboration between **Université Paris-Saclay** and partner universities in Vietnam. In 2026, the school is organized at:

- **VNU University of Science, Hanoi:** October 2–4, 2026
- **VNU-HCM University of Science, Ho Chi Minh City:** October 9–11, 2026
